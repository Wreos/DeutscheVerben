# Deutsche Verben

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Native SwiftUI for iPhone (iOS 18+) and Mac (macOS 15+).

## Users
Russian-speaking German learners practising short offline blocks.

## Product Purpose
Recall two principal parts and a context-specific auxiliary before seeing answers.

## Stack
SwiftUI app with a local dependency-free Swift package; versioned atomic JSON storage.

## Capabilities and Constraints
60 verbs from the existing web trainer, 10 per approach, A1/A2/B1 study groups, mistakes, rules/reference, per-verb learning scale and an SM-2 adaptation, drafts, paused approaches, original answers and help history, manual JSON transfer. No account, server, AI grading, automatic sync or CEFR inference. Original web trainer and learner data are read-only.

## Operating Context
Operate mode. Short sessions on iPhone, keyboard-led practice in resizable Mac windows. System navigation, readable German forms, Russian guidance. Routine UI decisions delegated by the user; inherit the incumbent green accent through native controls.

## Product Principles
Recall first; reveal only after submission or explicit reference/help. Assistance remains distinct from independent evidence. Imports preserve history and stop on conflicts. Data stays separate from lessons.

## Interface Copy

Learning screens contain task instructions and useful feedback. Avoid repeated CEFR caveats, technical storage details, scientific disclaimers and motivational filler. Keep concise assistance labels and the information needed to enter answers or transfer/restore progress safely. Full research and format details live in project documentation.
